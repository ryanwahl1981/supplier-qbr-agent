## Major issues and corrective action plans

### Responsiveness (score 1.0)
**What happened:** Issues answered within SLA dropped to 68% from 72% last quarter and 85% last year, declining steadily. Target is 80%.

**Likely cause:** Requests for quotes and delivery status went unanswered for extended periods. PO-0020 delivery date was never confirmed and buyer escalations went unanswered. (evidence: INC-013, INC-014)

**Corrective plan:** 30 days, due 2026-11-12. Checkpoints: 2026-10-28, 2026-11-12. Goal: category score back to 3.0 or higher.

- Establish and publish a 24-hour acknowledgment SLA for all customer requests by 2026-10-28.
- Submit a recovery plan to restore SLA compliance to 80% or higher by 2026-11-12.
- Name an escalation contact with authority to confirm delivery dates and respond to buyer requests by 2026-10-28.

**Supplier commitment needed:** Commit to 80% of issues answered within SLA by checkpoint 2026-10-28.
